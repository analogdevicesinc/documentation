ADRV936x Cable Brackets
===============================================================================

The U.FL connections on each of the :adi:`ADRV9361-Z7035` and
:adi:`ADRV9364-Z7020` PCBs are delicate and prone to damage from clumsy users.
In order to protect and preserve the board, a pair of cable brackets have been
designed to limit any external force on the connection. While nothing replaces
a careful and considerate user, these designs help to reduce any damage and
keep the U.FL cables plugged in.

Both designs were made in a free tool,
`Tinkercad <https://www.tinkercad.com>`_, and printed on several different
machines during development and prototype stages. The
`Selective Laser Sintering <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selective_laser_sintering>`_
done by `Sculpteo <http://www.sculpteo.com>`_ has produced good quality results
at reasonable prices.

Low Profile U.FL Cable Holder
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A simple design to protect the U.FL cable connections, printed to line up with
the mounting holes on the PCBs for a firm, secure connection. The cutouts in
the bottom of the print are designed to match the dimensions of the U.FL cable
when it is mated with the connector on the PCB.

L Bracket Cable Holder
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A more complex design than the cable holder. A series of mounting holes is
added so the SMA end of the U.FL cables can be mounted safely, preventing
dangling wires or connectors from shorting to ground and shutting down or
damaging the system.

   :width: 600
